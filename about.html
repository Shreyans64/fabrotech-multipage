About Us

Who we are & why clients choose Fabrotech

Fabrotech Engineers is a Nagpur-based manufacturer of outdoor recreational, fitness and civic infrastructure equipment. Founded in 2017 and registered as a Micro Enterprise (UDYAM-MH-20-0039469), we design, fabricate and supply from a single facility at MIDC Hingna Industrial Area — giving every order full material and process traceability.

Our partners built the company around one idea: the same fabrication discipline used on precision steel should apply to the equipment that parks, schools, malls and municipalities put in public use every day.

Why Fabrotech

Fabrication rigor, applied to public spaces
