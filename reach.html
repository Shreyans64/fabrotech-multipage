Roadmap

From Nagpur outward

A deliberate, phased route to reach — regional strength first, national scale next, export after that.

01 · NAGPUR / VIDARBHA

Home ground

Direct relationships, fastest turnaround, current core of order volume.

02 · MAHARASHTRA

State-wide

Municipal and private-developer projects across the state, building on the Nagpur base.

03 · PAN-INDIA

National, led by differentiated categories

Mall Trackless Trains and Science Park Exhibits — categories with fewer specialised competitors — lead national expansion, backed by ISO-certified manufacturing.

04 · GULF EXPORT

Longer-term target

ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 and EN 16630 are already in hand. BIS/ISI certification is the remaining step before export-ready status and government tender eligibility.
